## Relevant Industry Standards or Protocols

- **IATA NDC (New Distribution Capability)** — XML standard enabling direct airline content distribution to corporate booking tools, bypassing GDS intermediaries; NDC-enabled platforms report 6–12% cost savings
- **ISO 31030** — International standard for travel risk management, covering duty of care obligations, traveller safety protocols, and crisis response planning
- **PCI DSS** — Payment security standard governing corporate card and virtual card payment handling within travel booking and expense platforms
- **GDPR / CCPA** — Data privacy requirements governing traveller PNR data, expense data, and employee tracking for duty of care purposes
- **Global Business Travel Association (GBTA) Standards** — Industry body defining corporate travel programme benchmarking, supplier negotiation frameworks, and sustainability reporting standards
- **IATA BSP and Airline Reporting Corporation (ARC)** — Settlement systems used by TMCs for financial reconciliation with airlines; relevant to back-office platform compliance
- **Open Booking / Traveller Choice Framework** — Emerging industry approach to managed travel that accommodates direct supplier bookings within policy, requiring platforms to capture out-of-channel spend data

## Market Research

**Market Size:** The corporate travel management software market was valued at approximately USD 1.17–1.26 billion in 2025–2026, projected to reach USD 1.66 billion by 2030 at a CAGR of approximately 7.2%. This is distinct from the broader corporate travel spend market, which runs into hundreds of billions annually.

**Funding:** Navan (formerly TripActions) raised over $1.2 billion in venture and debt funding and was valued at $9.2 billion at its 2022 peak, though market conditions have moderated since. TravelPerk raised $104M Series D in 2022. SAP Concur remains a major business unit of SAP SE. Amex GBT went public via SPAC in 2022. The category has attracted significant capital due to its proximity to both corporate spending control and financial services.

**Pricing Landscape:** SAP Concur starts at approximately $9/user/month for basic travel, scaling significantly with expense and invoice modules. TravelPerk starts at $99/month with per-booking fees. Zoho Expense offers the lowest entry at $3/user/month. Engine uses a $450/year membership model with no booking fees. Enterprise platforms (Amex GBT, CWT, BCD) use custom pricing based on travel volume and service requirements. Companies using NDC-enabled platforms report savings of 6–12% on air spend.

**Key Buyer Personas:** Corporate travel managers and programme owners at companies spending $1M+ annually on business travel; CFOs and finance directors seeking to reduce travel spend and improve expense visibility; HR managers responsible for duty of care and traveller safety; procurement directors negotiating supplier agreements and managing programme compliance.

**Notable Trends:** The 2026 corporate travel landscape is defined by conversational AI booking assistants, NDC airline connections, proactive intelligence feeds, and unified travel-plus-expense platforms. AI booking agents can now search, compare, confirm, and expense-code a full trip (flights via NDC, hotels, car) in a single conversational exchange. NDC adoption continues to grow as airlines retire legacy GDS agreements, with TMCs serving as critical aggregators normalising content across multiple carriers. Duty of care capabilities — real-time traveller location tracking and emergency communication — have become table-stakes following the pandemic.

## AI-Native Opportunity

- Conversational AI travel booking that allows employees to request, modify, and confirm complete trips through natural language, with the system automatically checking policy compliance, applying preferred supplier rates, and generating expense pre-codes — eliminating the traditional booking tool UI entirely
- Proactive travel risk monitoring that continuously tracks traveller locations against real-time safety intelligence feeds and triggers automated duty-of-care alerts and rebooking options when disruptions or security events occur
- Spend pattern analysis that identifies policy leakage, preferred supplier compliance gaps, and unused negotiated discounts across travel and expense data, surfacing savings recommendations directly to programme managers
- AI-driven policy optimisation that analyses actual traveller booking behaviour, programme costs, and satisfaction data to recommend policy adjustments that balance cost control with traveller experience
- Supplier negotiation intelligence that aggregates historical booking volumes, category spend, and market rate benchmarks to model the financial impact of proposed supplier agreements before they are signed
